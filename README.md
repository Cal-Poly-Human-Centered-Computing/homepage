# Cal Poly Human-Centered Computing Group

We are faculty and students at Cal Poly San Luis Obispo who study how people use, understand, and are affected by computing. Our work includes machine learning, accessibility, data visualization, computing education, social computing, and responsible AI.

Website: https://cal-poly-human-centered-computing.github.io/homepage/

Want to add yourself, a project, or fix something? See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

This website and group are informally maintained by the affiliated faculty and students and do not officially represent Cal Poly San Luis Obispo.
