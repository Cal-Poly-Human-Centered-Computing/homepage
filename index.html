Cal Poly Human-Centered Computing Group

We are faculty and students at Cal Poly San Luis Obispo who study how people use, understand, and are affected by computing. Our work includes machine learning, accessibility, data visualization, computing education, social computing, and responsible AI.

        Sponsors

          Sponsors and collaborators help to set our research directions, and also receive access to team members and early results. If you are interested in sponsoring or collaborating with us, from industry, government, or a non-profit, please contact one of the faculty members or project leads listed above. If you're unsure who would be most appropriate, send email to felavsky@calpoly.edu, who will help to direct your mail to the right place.
